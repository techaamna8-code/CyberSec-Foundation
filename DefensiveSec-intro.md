## 🛠️ Key Takeaways for Blue Teaming / SOC
- **Log Visibility:** Centralized log inspection is critical for early threat detection.
- **Triage & Response:** Fast identification and immediate containment prevent lateral movement and data loss.
- **Rule Enforcement:** Quick rule updates on firewalls and network perimeters are crucial during active incidents.
